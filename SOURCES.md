
# Cultural Sources

This document records the sources used to verify cultural information
for The Light We Carry.

## 1. Nepal Tourism Board – Tihar

- URL: https://ntb.gov.np/tihar
- Used for:
  - The five-day Tihar framework.
  - Kaag Tihar and the association of crows with Yama.
  - Kukur Tihar and the worship of dogs.
  - Gai Tihar and Laxmi Puja.
  - Goru Tihar and Bhai Tika traditions.

## 2. Nepal Tourism Board – Mha Puja

- URL: https://ntb.gov.np/en/mha-puja
- Used for:
  - Mha Puja as a Newar observance.
  - Worship and celebration of the self.
  - Its association with Nepal Sambat and the Newar New Year.

## 3. Radio Nepal – Tihar festival concludes: sisters, brothers exchange tika on final day

- URL: https://radionepalonline.com/en/2025/10/23/418638.html
- Used for:
  - Bhai Tika traditions.
  - Sisters offering tika and blessings to brothers.
  - Reciprocal tika and blessings between siblings.

## Content review notes

- Tihar traditions and observance dates can vary between communities and years.
- Mha Puja is a distinct Newar observance, not another name for Goru Tihar.
- Nepali spellings and wording should be reviewed by a fluent Nepali speaker before publication.
- Cultural descriptions should remain respectful and avoid suggesting that every community observes traditions identically.
